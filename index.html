01.About Me

Hello! My name is Rafiu and I build reliable, production-ready systems at the boundary where modern AI models meet real-world software engineering. My journey began with core algorithmic problem solving and low-level computer science at Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET).

Rather than simply calling third-party cloud APIs, my engineering philosophy treats neural networks as probabilistic system components. In high-stakes environments—such as real-time proctoring or high-volume examinations—every probabilistic model inference must be coupled with strict schema validation, constrained sampling, and immediate deterministic algorithmic fallback paths.

Recently, I have engineered full-stack proctored examination platforms with local SLM error analysis, in-browser optical mark recognition using C++ OpenCV compiled to WebAssembly, and cross-platform network interception engines in Go.
